Privacy Policy for Nopubly

Effective Date: January 17, 2026

1. Overview

Nopubly ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how our app operates regarding your data.

2. Data Collection & VPN Service

Nopubly uses the Android VpnService specifically to create a local "sinkhole" on your device. This allows us to block connections to known adware and tracking domains.

  • No External Logging: We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your web traffic, DNS queries, or IP address to any external server. All traffic processing happens locally on your device.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, emails, or phone numbers.

3. Permissions

We request permissions solely to provide functionality:

  • Query All Packages: To scan installed apps for known risks (Spyware/Adware).
  • Foreground Service: To keep the protection active.

If you have questions, contact us at: support@nopubly.com
