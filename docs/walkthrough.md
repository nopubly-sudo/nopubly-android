# Nopubly Adware & Privacy Update

Values: **Safety**, **Control**, **Transparency**.

I have enhanced Nopubly to effectively target adware, **Location Trackers**, **Spyware**, and now **Viral Growth**.

## Key Changes

### 1. 🔍 Cleaner-First Design
- **Redesign**: The app is now a dedicated **Cleaner Tool**.
- **Hero Button**: A massive "SCAN NOW" button takes center stage.
- **Pro Shield**: The real-time VPN is now a secondary "Pro" feature.

### 2. ☣️ Signature-Based Detection (Enterprise Grade)
- **Problem**: Some malware pretends to be innocent and passes heuristic checks.
- **Solution**: We now check against a database of **Known Malware**.
- **Visuals**: Marked with a **"☣️ MALWARE"** (Red) badge. **CRITICAL RISK**.
- **Action**: Immediate uninstall recommended.

### 3. 🛡️ Spyware Detector (Microphone & Camera)
- **Problem**: Apps that can spy on you without you knowing.
- **Solution**: The scanner now audits **Microphone** and **Camera** permissions.
- **Visuals**: 
    - **"🎤 MIC"** (Amber): App can record audio.
    - **"📷 CAM"** (Pink): App can take photos/video.
- **Impact**: Increased Risk Score (+20 points each).

### 4. 📢 Viral Sharing (Growth Engine)
- **Mechanism**: After a scan, users are prompted to **"Share Result"**.
- **Social Proof**: 
    - If Clean: "My phone is safe thanks to Nopubly!" (Pride).
    - If Infected: "I found spyware! Check yours." (Urgency).
- **Goal**: Turn every user into a promoter.

### 5. 📍 Location Tracking Detection
- **Visuals**: marked with a **"📍 RASTREADOR"** badge (Purple).

### 6. ⚠️ Emergency Overlay Fix
- **Solution**: **"⚠️ DETENER INTERFERENCIAS"** button.

### 7. 🌍 Internationalization
- App now fully supports **English** and **Spanish** automatically based on your device settings.

## How to Test
1.  **Rebuild**: `yarn android`
2.  **Scan**: Look for the **Spyware** badges.
3.  **Share**: Press the share button in the alert dialog to verify the social message.
